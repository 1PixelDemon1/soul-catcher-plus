# Soul Raven alternate form

The white-headed long-tailed tit uses stepped white cheeks and crown, a dark nape and sloped back, dusty pink shoulders and a three-bar tail with a wider root. The back and far side follow the symmetry of the plumage. The cyan amulet is attached to the breast below the head pivot.

The model uses the same forward direction, floor origin and nine named bones as Soul Raven. The target size is one Minecraft block (16 × 16 × 16 Blockbench units), including the tail and wings through the perch and flight loops. The tit has shorter legs and a lower head. Its flight tail track adjusts for the longer, more steeply posed tail.

Run `python modeling/soul-long-tailed-tit/generate_model.py` from the repository root to rebuild the plan, swatches and editable project. The `.bbmodel` embeds its material swatches for portability.
