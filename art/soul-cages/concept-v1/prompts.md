# Soul cage concept prompts

Designs generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool during the preview-only stage. Final PNG exports are exactly 16x16 with nearest-pixel sampling and binary alpha. Gameplay integration followed the user's approval.

The user rejected the original cubical barred-cage design and supplied a glass-vessel sketch. The diamond shape became the family reference, with intermediate body width, smaller chain links and a third link added on top.
