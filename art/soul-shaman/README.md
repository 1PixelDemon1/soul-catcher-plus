# Soul Shaman — concept art

Status: first design draft for user review. This is art only; no Blockbench model,
entity, gameplay code or game resources are added by this design task.

`concept-v1.png` is the unchanged output of the built-in image generation tool.
The sheet shows one large three-quarter view and smaller front/back views.

## Proposed visual direction

- Small hovering companion with a short charcoal/teal hood and broad cloak panels.
- Elongated ivory bone mask with rectangular cyan eyes and sparse dark cuts.
- Coarse blue cloth accents matching the Forgotten Souls family.
- Angular wooden staff with a bone fork, blue soul flame and one bone charm.
- Cyan chest amulet and a short spirit wisp instead of visible feet.
- Large readable shapes and restrained texture detail for later Minecraft modeling.

The user's gameplay brief is a support soul for its owner and nearby allied souls,
possibly Strength I and Regeneration I within a small owner-centred radius.
Exact model dimensions, health, range and balance remain to be agreed before
implementation. Visual details on this sheet are proposals pending approval.
