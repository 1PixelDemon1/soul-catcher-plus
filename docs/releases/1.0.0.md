# Soul Catcher Plus 1.0.0

Release date: **2026-10-31**. First major release, with separate core and combined integration-addon builds for Forge **1.16.5** and **1.20.1**.

Capture defeated enemies as permanent collection entries, store energy in portable soul cages, and summon the captured forms as spectral companions. This release adds four equipment tiers, three enchantments, group and aggressive commands, collection search and form deletion, advancements, and a separately installable integration addon.

## Install the build for your Minecraft version

| Minecraft | Forge baseline | Game runtime | Core artifact | Optional integration addon |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.16.5 | 36.2.42 | Java 8 | `soulcatcherplus-1.0.0+1.16.5.jar` | `soulcatcherplus-compat-1.0.0+1.16.5.jar` |
| 1.20.1 | 47.4.28 | Java 17 | `soulcatcherplus-1.0.0+1.20.1.jar` | `soulcatcherplus-compat-1.0.0+1.20.1.jar` |

Install the chosen core on both client and server. Install the addon on both sides if you need its integrations, using the **same full version and Minecraft suffix** as the core. The core runs without any creature mod or animation library. Creature mods and their required libraries are separate installations; they are not bundled into either JAR.

These four artifacts are separate builds. Do not install both Minecraft variants in one profile or use 1.16.5 files on 1.20.1. Fabric, NeoForge, and the separate 1.19.2/1.20.4 development ports are outside this release.

## Capture, energy, and equipment

Right-click an enemy with a scythe in Harvest mode to mark it for three minutes, defeat it yourself or through a companion, then collect its owner-bound soul remnant within one minute. The collection belongs to the player's UUID, independently of the scythe. New appearances unlock captured forms within a species; capturing a known form grants no second energy reward.

Soul energy now belongs to individual **soul cage items**. Cages are non-stackable and retain their contents when transferred or upgraded. Losing a cage loses its energy. Marking, capturing, and summoning require a cage in the offhand or hotbar; menu viewing and orders need no cage. Collection itself is free, so even an empty or full cage permits capture.

| Tier | Base cage capacity | Scythe companion limit | Attack damage | Attacks per second |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Iron | 150 | 1 | 8 | 0.7 |
| Diamond | 400 | 2 | 8 | 0.8 |
| Netherite | 1000 | 3 | 9 | 0.8 |
| Supreme | 2000 | 4 | 10 | 0.8 |

The active cage is the offhand cage, or otherwise the leftmost hotbar cage. A summon's entire price must fit in that one cage. An empty or undersized active cage does not automatically switch to another; move it to choose another cage. Main-inventory and armor slots do not participate. Victory rewards and recall returns fill the offhand and then the hotbar from left to right, spilling into available space. Overflow, or energy earned without an eligible cage, is lost.

The economy uses health points, not hearts:

- Summon cost: `ceil(captured maximum HP * 2)`, at least 1.
- Base victory energy: `floor(maximum HP / 2)`, at least 1.
- Recall return: `floor(current HP / 2)`, at least 0, for a living, loaded root companion only. Dead, missing, unloaded, or child mobs have no independent return; recalls do not load chunks for a refund.

Iron and diamond scythes use two matching materials, a soul lantern, and two sticks. Supreme scythes use two bones, a Nether Star, and two sticks. Iron/diamond cages use five matching materials, a chain, two glass, and soul sand; the supreme cage substitutes bones and a Nether Star. Netherite upgrades use a diamond item and netherite ingot at a smithing table. **Only 1.20.1 also requires a netherite upgrade template.** Cage energy survives upgrading.

## Enchantments and progression

- **Soul Capacity I–III** applies to cages. Each level adds one third of the base capacity, rounding down once. At level III, capacities are 300/800/2000/4000.
- **Soul Harvester I–II** applies to held scythes and increases credited victory energy by 50%/100%, rounding down. The stronger held enchantment wins without stacking. Companion kills can benefit; summon prices, recall returns, and remnant appearance do not change.
- **Soul Slot I** is a rare armor enchantment. Each worn piece adds one companion slot, up to four; a supreme scythe and a full enchanted set allow eight companions. Inventory armor gives no bonus. A held scythe remains necessary for a new summon.

Enchanting and enchanted books use the normal enchanting/anvil systems. No dedicated enchanted-book loot injection is added. Scythes also accept normal compatible sword enchantments. Removing equipment or switching to a weaker scythe does not dismiss companions already active; the next summon uses the current limit, with the main-hand scythe taking precedence.

Five advancements cover joining a world, first soul capture, obtaining both supreme items, capturing a soul with at least 150 maximum HP, and reaching eight available companion slots. The last condition checks capacity; it does not require eight simultaneous summons.

## Collection and companion controls

Hold any soul scythe in either hand to open **O** for the collection or hold **R** for the command wheel. Both controls are remappable. Unlike 0.2.0, these menus now require a held scythe. Right-click scythe marking and targeted attack orders remain available through Harvest/Command mode.

The collection groups captured forms by species and keeps species-wide favorites, detached rotatable/zoomable previews, and English/Russian text. A scrolling list and name/ID search replace paging. Search text and cursor survive server updates. **Delete form** asks for confirmation and removes only the selected form, recalling it first if active through the normal return/cleanup rules. Other forms remain, and the deleted form can be captured again. A species loses its favorite flag only when its final form is removed.

The wheel supports individual companions and **All**, with Follow, Aggressive, Wait, Guard, and Recall. It shows the active cage, live loaded-companion HP, and orders. Wheel selection does not change collection selection or group-command scope. The wheel keeps the world running; the full collection pauses unpublished singleplayer worlds, with recovery measured in world time.

Aggressive companions follow their owner and select eligible visible hostiles within 16 blocks of both themselves and the owner. Default hostility includes ordinary monster types and creatures attacking the owner; adapters can refine it. In Follow and Aggressive modes, exceeding 24 blocks from the owner clears combat and attempts a safe return every five server ticks, independently of native attack goals. If no safe space exists, later checks retry.

The 1.20.1 port also corrects its follow-goal repath delay for the goal's reduced tick cadence. Its medium-soul creative icon is explicitly included in the item atlas.

One active companion per species and a shared 60-second recovery after defeat remain in effect. Losing a companion does not remove its captured form. Loaded living recalls can return energy; player death, logout, and dimension changes use the same lifecycle rules.

## Soul artwork and effects

Remnants are fullbright floating soul lanterns: small below 20 base victory energy, medium from 20 through 40, and large above 40. A captured maximum HP of at least 150 selects a flaming skull instead, and highlights that species and preview title in purple. This threshold is health-based, not a classification of every modded boss.

Cages animate three souls with four 16×16 frames, transparent glass, and chain handles; their animation is decorative even when empty. Supreme equipment uses bones and a Nether Star. Scythes have refreshed inventory icons, 64×64 held textures, closed meshes, and shared material/grip conventions. A dedicated creative tab and mod logo use the medium soul lantern.

The core retains dispatcher-level spectral tint/transparency and client-only departure echoes that dissolve over 18 ticks on recall or death. Server companion cleanup does not wait for the effect. Custom renderer buffers, materials, shaders, and layers can require dedicated visual integration.

## Combined integration addon

`Soul Catcher Plus: Mod Integrations` (`soulcatcherplus_compat`) is one optional addon containing the following corrections. Registrations activate only when the corresponding creature mod is present. Removing the addon leaves collection IDs intact, but removes its special behavior corrections.

| Integration | Minecraft 1.16.5 baseline | Minecraft 1.20.1 baseline | Included correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mowzie's Mobs | 1.5.27; addon metadata requires this exact version | 1.8.2 API/bytecode inspected; build profile pins GeckoLib 4.8.3; adapter metadata allows 1.6+ | Wroughtnaut activation/appearance and area-attack ally filtering |
| Twilight Forest | 4.0.870 | 4.3.2508 | Snow Queen ice-crystal attribution and native master links for Lich clones/minions |
| Aquamirae | 5.4.0 / API 11 | 6.1.2 and 6.4.0 API/bytecode inspected | Preserve Cornelia's shared music cooldown for ordinary bosses when the source is a companion |

These are build/source/API baselines, not a declaration that every creature or gameplay interaction is tested. Mowzie's Mobs 1.5.27 and 1.8.2 integration methods/fields were checked against their actual APIs; the 1.8.2 review also checked the area-query Mixin descriptors and appearance tags. The 1.16.5 Aquamirae development profile also pins Obscure API 11 and GeckoLib 3.0.106; install a creature mod's own version-appropriate libraries separately. Aquamirae 7.x has a different implementation and is not covered by the inspected 6.x hook. A broad optional dependency range is not proof of compatibility with every admitted version.

The **core** retains generic capture/summoning, commands, ownership, spectral rendering, boss-bar discovery, music-source filtering, and public adapter/events/visual APIs. Identifiable companion boss music is suppressed without globally muting music or ordinary sounds; shared encounter controllers, asynchronous music, or sounds without an identifiable source can need an adapter.

Minion inheritance follows explicit creator links or opt-in spawn attribution, never proximity alone. Recognized children inherit allegiance, permitted targets, spectral appearance, and root cleanup while retaining native movement/attacks. They consume no collection entries or slots and grant no loot, XP, soul energy, or independent recall return. Their credited enemy kills belong to the player.

The core restores native summoner links before AI, supports adapter-approved children constructed during a summoner tick, and persists root ownership. When a valid root is unloaded, its child's native server tick is paused, including native lifetime timers. Root recall/death/removal dismisses loaded descendants and invalidates unloaded ones for cleanup on reload. Previously unlinked minions cannot reliably be adopted retroactively; newly created summons are needed for those links. These rules do not cover every custom spell, projectile, Brain, transformation, or boss phase.

## Upgrade and migration

Back up an existing world before updating. Update client and server core builds together, and update any installed addon to its exact matching core version. Both release lines use **protocol 11**; 0.2.0 used protocol 6. Collection synchronization now sends snapshots in parts of at most 32 rows, applying a snapshot only after all its rows arrive.

The ledger writes schema 3 and retains compatible collection identities, captured forms, favorites, active reservations, and recovery data within the same Minecraft line. **Old player-ledger energy is not converted into cage energy.** Obtain cages and earn new energy after updating. The original `soul_scythe` item ID remains the iron scythe. Removing a creature mod can leave saved entries unavailable until that mod returns.

No cross-Minecraft world or collection migration tool is provided. Similar IDs and features do not make a 1.20.1 world safe to open in 1.16.5, or establish a supported transfer in the other direction. Use separate game profiles and worlds for the two builds. External integrations must compile against the corresponding Minecraft build and updated public APIs; this milestone does not guarantee binary compatibility with pre-1.0 adapters.

## Packaging and validation

Release packaging completed successfully for **both** source trees with `.\scripts\gradle.ps1 build -x test`. The normal release source sets produced all four reobfuscated runtime JARs without QA sources. For 1.16.5, Gradle/javac run on Java 17 and Jabel 1.0.0 emits Java 8 bytecode with `--release 8`; Jabel is compile-only and is not included in the mod. The 1.20.1 build targets Java 17.

Artifact inspection passed archive integrity, JSON parsing, metadata/version checks, and core/addon isolation for all four JARs. Class versions are 52 for both 1.16.5 JARs and 61 for both 1.20.1 JARs. Each core contains five advancements. No bundled Jabel, manual QA helpers, or nested third-party JARs were found. The 1.20.1 manual QA command was compiled separately after API adjustments; that did not execute tests and does not put it in the release artifacts.

The local release output is `build/releases/1.0.0`, with version-specific subdirectories, `artifact-audit.json`, and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. These are the four project artifacts; the separate development playtest bundle with third-party dependencies is not the public release package. The [validation record](1.0.0-validation.md) identifies the source revisions and completed checks without treating later documentation commits as gameplay changes.

At the user's request, no release Minecraft client/server was launched and no automated tests, GameTests, or UI automation were run. Minecraft 1.16.5 has no supported GameTest task in this project. Earlier manual user reports describe their development snapshots; they are not final-artifact validation or evidence that the 1.20.1 port has been gameplay-tested. Source review, compilation, and archive inspection establish different facts from playing the release.

## Remaining limits

This major milestone does not promise universal mod compatibility. Standard goal-based mobs are the primary generic path; complex AI, source-less summons, custom rendering, shared boss bars/music, transformations, and encounter-wide state can need dedicated adapters. Boss-bar discovery remains heuristic, and suppressing one participant of a shared event may be unsafe.

The collection has a 4096-entry bound. Only one form per species can be active at once. Multiplayer across two real clients, long-running saves, every creature/version combination, exceptional cleanup paths, and renderer/shader combinations have not received comprehensive release validation. Source-based minion performance analysis is not a benchmark. Balance and integration interfaces may evolve in later releases.
