# Soul Catcher Plus 0.1.0

First tagged pre-1.0 milestone for **Minecraft Java 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.28 / Java 17**. Release tag: `v0.1.0`. Distributable mod version: `0.1.0+1.20.1`.

Mark an enemy with a soul scythe, defeat it, collect its soul, and spend energy to summon the original creature as a spectral companion. Collections and energy belong to the player's UUID and persist independently of the weapon.

## Gameplay

- A mark lasts three minutes. After a credited victory, its owner has one minute to collect the remnant with a scythe.
- Species are grouped in the collection; capturing a new appearance unlocks another form. Only one form of a species can be active at a time, and forms share a 60-second recovery cooldown after defeat.
- Companions can follow, wait, guard an area, attack a chosen target, or be recalled. Commands apply to the selected species or the group.
- Summons retain their original entity type and native abilities where supported. The default controller targets ordinary goal-based mobs; the optional Ferrous Wroughtnaut adapter preserves its native combat and activation and filters allies from area attacks.
- Server checks enforce ownership, energy, summon limits, and active identities. Companion deaths do not produce duplicated loot or energy.

## Three scythe tiers and the health economy

| Scythe | Maximum companions | Attack damage | Attacks per second |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Iron | 1 | 8 | 0.7 |
| Diamond | 2 | 8 | 0.8 |
| Netherite | 3 | 9 | 0.8 |

The held scythe is checked when creating a new summon; changing weapons does not revoke companions already active. A main-hand scythe takes precedence over an offhand scythe. Every companion counts as one, including bosses.

Summon cost is `ceil(captured maximum HP * 2)`. Credited victory energy is `floor(maximum HP / 2)`. Both have a minimum of one, and HP means health points rather than hearts. A 20-HP mob therefore yields 10 energy and costs 40 to summon. Collecting an already owned appearance grants no additional energy beyond the victory reward.

Iron and diamond recipes use this layout:

```text
material  material  soul lantern
empty     stick     empty
stick     empty     empty
```

Use two iron ingots for iron or two diamonds for diamond. Netherite is a smithing upgrade: a **diamond soul scythe**, **one netherite ingot**, and a **netherite upgrade smithing template**. The netherite item is fire resistant.

## Collection interface and visuals

The English/Russian collection screen uses ink, aged brass, and teal panels with species cards, captured-form selection, resource and HP indicators, and contextual commands. Its detached mob preview rotates automatically and supports drag rotation and scroll-wheel zoom; framing reserves space for wide bodies, weapons, and horns.

The screen pauses singleplayer worlds that have not been shared over LAN. Recovery displays follow world time, while session-only network throttles keep menu requests available during the pause.

Summoned bodies use spectral rendering with standard living-entity renderers. Death and recall trigger a short client-only echo that dissolves from feet to head over 18 ticks, with fading, soul-fire particles, and a quiet escape sound. The server still removes combat entities through its existing lifecycle. Particles and sound remain available if the native model cannot be drawn as an echo.

Each material has a 16x16 inventory sprite and separate 128x128 held artwork. Small icons also serve dropped items and frames. Enlarged held models use closed opaque meshes, a centered shaft grip, and independent depth scaling to remain one model pixel thick. Editable artwork sources and regeneration instructions are included in `art/scythes`.

Entity-owned companion boss bars are suppressed without removing encounter participants. Automatic discovery inspects direct boss-event fields and is heuristic; adapters can supply helper-owned events or exclude shared bars.

## Installation and migration

Install `soulcatcherplus-0.1.0+1.20.1.jar` in the `mods` folder of both client and server with Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47.4.28. The base mod does not require Mowzie's Mobs. For the Wroughtnaut integration, install **Mowzie's Mobs 1.8.2 for 1.20.1** and **GeckoLib 4.8.3 for Forge 1.20.1** separately. Their code and assets are not bundled.

This release uses network protocol **4**. Update both sides together; the earlier prototype protocols are incompatible even though development artifacts also used the 0.1.0 version label.

The ledger now writes schema 2. Older entries infer captured maximum HP from saved attributes, including saved modifiers; if unavailable, half the old summon cost is a best-effort fallback. Ownership, energy, collection snapshots, selection, active identities, and recovery data are retained. Old weighted slots are no longer used. The iron scythe keeps `soulcatcherplus:soul_scythe`, preserving its registry identity.

Datapack soul profiles now control capture availability only. Legacy `cost`, `slots`, and `energy` profile fields are ignored in favor of the shared HP economy and scythe limits. Public adapter, event, preview, boss-bar, and rendering interfaces remain experimental; see [API documentation](../API.md). See the [README](../../README.md) for controls and development commands.

## Known limitations

This is a pre-1.0 milestone with provisional balance and public interfaces. Brain-based AI, custom encounter phases, minions, unusual renderers, and other special behavior can require dedicated adapters. Compatibility with arbitrary modpacks is not guaranteed.

Shared or manager-owned boss bars and custom HUDs need integration support. Spectral layers, departure echoes, and previews depend on native renderer behavior; third-party shaders and unusual rendering paths have not been comprehensively checked. The Wroughtnaut retains its native special defense and can be exceptionally powerful.

Two real multiplayer clients, long-running worlds, and the final collection of integrations have not received comprehensive release testing. There is no companion progression tree or support for other Minecraft versions/loaders in this release.
