# Soul Catcher Plus 1.3.0-prerelease

Release date: **2026-11-21**. This is a **core-only prerelease** for four Forge
platforms. Compat is excluded. Build, archive, and isolated server-startup checks
passed; clients and gameplay were not tested for this release.

## Soul devices

**Soul Pylon.** Insert a charged cage and a soul scroll to summon a block-bound
guardian. Summoning spends the stored form's energy cost. Guardians use a separate
world reservation, not the owner's personal companion slots. Ordinary mobs using
the standard companion brain and Raven are supported; custom-brain companions are
not automatically eligible. New summons require the owner online. Guardians use
the loaded anchor, and an unloaded entity is not treated as dead or replaced.
Pylons do not keep chunks loaded. Their item slots are edited through the menu;
hopper insertion/extraction is not supported.

**Raven stockpiles.** A pylon Raven gathers nearby item entities and deposits them
beside its anchor, preferring a safe adjacent hopper. It avoids collecting its own
stockpile repeatedly and retains cargo while its anchor is unloaded. This mode
does not collect soul remnants; a personal Raven retains its owner-delivery role.

**Soul Projector and Quartz Soul Projector.** Place a scroll inside to display a
detached, decorative version of its creature. Set size (25–400%), angle, color,
opacity (10–100%), and automatic rotation. The display is not spawned into the
world or AI-ticked and has independent appearance settings.

**Soul Transfer Altar.** Place paper and a charged cage, select a form from your
collection, and pay the displayed cost to inscribe a scroll. The collection entry
remains available, allowing the scroll to be shared with another player. The altar
uses its own inserted cage and requires an empty output slot.

**Soul-Fueled Furnace.** Smelt ordinary furnace recipes with an inserted cage,
spending one soul energy per operation. Recipe cooking time and experience remain
recipe-driven; paid progress is saved with the block.

## Soul-powered equipment

| Equipment | Energy cost | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Soul Energy Bow | 10 per shot | Fires a soul bolt without consuming arrows; retains draw strength and applicable bow enchantments. |
| Soulbound Hoe | 5 per successful fertilization | Sneak-use applies bone-meal behavior; an unsuccessful application returns its energy payment. Ordinary hoe use remains available. |
| Soul Sacrifice Totem of Undying | 500 per revival | Hold in either hand for a reusable revival when vanilla totem protection has not already handled the hit. Bypass-invulnerability damage remains excluded. |

Equipment searches the offhand followed by the hotbar for the first cage that can
pay the entire action. It does not pool energy across cages. These equipment rules
do not change the ordinary companion-summoning active-cage selection.

The Soul Energy Bow and Soulbound Hoe smithing recipes require the vanilla
**Netherite Upgrade** template on 1.20.1 and 26.3. The 1.16.5/1.19.2 variants use
their older smithing format without a template.

## Forms, appearance, and orders

Use wheat seeds on your active Raven to unlock its **Long-tailed Tit** form, or a
note block on your active Shaman to unlock **Hatsune Miku**. These are additional
collection forms of existing companions, retaining their respective roles rather
than introducing new combat species. The interaction does not replace the active
companion. Forgotten Soul models/materials are refreshed, including the Courier's
translucent chest rendering and form-specific geometry.

Soul color and opacity are saved per owner and synchronized to observers. The
appearance picker retains the 10–100% opacity range and 42% default; decorative
projectors keep their own settings. Recognized flying souls now hold their
position during Wait, while ordinary ground mobs retain gravity.

The optional English/Russian Patchouli guide grows to **25 entries and six
categories per language**, with functional blocks, altar copying, equipment, and
recipe pages. Matching older-platform Patchouli examples remain 53.3/77/85 for
1.16.5/1.19.2/1.20.1. No working published Forge Patchouli build is verified for
26.3, so guide availability or book crafting there is not claimed. Its JAR still
includes the translated guide text resources.

## Installation and compatibility

Install the core for the selected Minecraft version on both client and server.
The new network protocol is **17**, replacing 1.2.3's protocol 13; both sides must
run the matching prerelease. A shared protocol number never enables connections
between different Minecraft versions.

No creature mod, animation library, or Compat addon is required by the core.
This release does not update or certify the separately distributed Compat addon
against the prerelease. Generic adapters and preview APIs do not establish
universal mod support, especially for custom AI or renderers. Creature integrations
and Forge Patchouli on 26.3 remain unverified.

Back up worlds before trying the prerelease and keep version-specific profiles.
New devices, pylon reservations, and owner appearance add saved data; no rollback
guarantee or universal migration between Minecraft versions is supplied. Historical
release artifacts and tags remain unchanged.

## Artifacts and validation

Shareable files are one runtime JAR and one sources JAR per platform:
`soulcatcherplus-1.3.0-prerelease+<minecraft>.jar` and
`soulcatcherplus-1.3.0-prerelease+<minecraft>-sources.jar`. Only the runtime JAR is
installed. All eight JARs, exact checksums in `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and
`archive-audit.json` are frozen in `build/releases/1.3.0-prerelease`.
Development runs, private playtest bundles, and external dependencies are not
public runtime artifacts.

All four fresh `build -x test` runs passed. Archive audits verified ZIP CRCs and
unique paths, exact Minecraft/Forge metadata, Java class versions 52/61/61/69,
parseable JSON and guide recipe references, new recipes/models/localized forms,
and Mixin entries. Runtime archives exclude QA, Compat, Jabel, and JUnit.

Isolated local Forge development servers reached **Done** on all four versions
and saved/stopped gracefully with exit 0. The final 26.3 run followed the
advancement-codec correction; subsequent 26.3 changes were limited to client render
extraction/cache cleanup and were not exercised by a client launch.

No clients, automated tests, GameTests, UI automation, gameplay, or multiplayer
verification ran for this release. Server startup does not establish installed
production-client startup or verify guardian lifecycle, automation, combat,
equipment revival, visuals, or multiplayer behavior. Earlier release checks are
historical evidence only.
